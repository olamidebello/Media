Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Version 2026-10-25

Scope

This notice describes the current Africa Media Hub implementation, version 2026-10-25. It applies to our service, not independent providers shown in links, embedded panels or app browser views.

Information handled

Accounts store an email address, a password hash, role and active status. We store profile names and Kids settings; saved titles, playlists, viewing progress, reactions, comments, reports and recording requests; creator media URLs and metadata; administrator audit events; and legal request contact details and messages. Hosting infrastructure may log IP addresses, request paths, device headers, timestamps and errors. Do not include passwords or payment-card details in submissions.

Purpose and access

We use information to authenticate accounts, operate viewing and creator tools, remember preferences, moderate content, maintain security, respond to requests and comply with law. Administrators and hosting or service providers may process the data needed for those tasks. We do not implement an advertising-data sale or targeted-advertising system in this release; independent providers may have different practices.

Location and device detection

Country discovery requests browser permission before obtaining coordinates. Rounded coordinates are sent directly from your browser to OpenStreetMap Nominatim for country lookup. The application server does not store those coordinates. Your selected country is saved locally on the device and can be cleared. Device classification uses browser information locally and may be inaccurate. Selecting a country manually does not require sharing GPS coordinates.

Cookies and local storage

Session cookies maintain login and are HttpOnly with expiry. Device storage remembers country, TV mode and playback preferences. An installed web app may cache an offline notice and icon. The application service worker does not intentionally cache account API responses or video. Signing out removes the session; clearing site data removes local preferences and may sign you out.

External viewing

Opening a provider panel, YouTube player, native browser view or remote media URL can disclose your IP address, browser or device information, referring origin and viewing activity to that provider. Providers may set cookies, collect additional information, require an account or process payments. Their notices and your device/browser controls apply. Embedding is not a guarantee of privacy separation or an affiliation.

Retention and security

Active account and activity data remain until removed through administrative operations or a verified request, unless a lawful security or recordkeeping need requires retention. Backups may retain older copies until their rotation or removal. There is no automatic fixed retention or deletion schedule in the current release. Password hashing, access controls and transport security reduce risk but do not make transmission or storage risk-free.

Children

Accounts are intended for adults who manage family use. A Kids profile does not establish parental consent. This release has no verifiable parental-consent process. Please do not submit children’s personal information. Parents or guardians can report an unintended collection through the legal contact form for review and removal where appropriate.

Your choices and requests

Use account and viewing settings, sign out, clear local device data, or decline location permission. Request access, correction, deletion, export, or restriction through the legal contact page. We may verify account ownership before acting. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on your location; the service does not claim automatic compliance with every jurisdiction. We do not charge or require unnecessary information merely to submit a request.

Cross-border processing and updates

Your information may be processed where the operator, hosting infrastructure and external providers operate. Specific processing locations and operator contact information can be requested through the legal contact form. We will date material updates and provide additional notice or consent where required.

Marketplace and account groups

Administrators can organize accounts into groups and manage their roles, status and profiles. Marketplace orders store account and product references, the purchase price and currency, delivery instructions, payment provider references, timestamps and payment status. Checkout sends your account email and order information to the selected Stripe or Paystack service. Enter payment details on the provider’s hosted page; this platform does not collect card numbers. Providers process payment information under their own privacy policies. Purchase records may remain after account removal with the account association removed.
